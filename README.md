# Outlook Ring Alert 🔔

An Android application designed for "Mission Critical" Outlook email monitoring. This app ensures you never miss important emails during specific hours (like late at night) by turning regular notifications into persistent, phone-call style ringtones.



## 🚀 Key Features

- **Customizable Time Window**: Set specific hours (e.g., 12 AM to 6 AM) when you want the app to be active. It supports midnight crossover logic.
- **Reliability Dashboard**: A real-time checklist that monitors system settings that could interfere with alerts:
    - Notification Listener Access
    - Do Not Disturb (DND) status
    - System Ringer Volume
    - Battery Optimization status
- **Activity Log**: A "Black Box" diagnostic log that shows exactly why the app did or did not ring for every incoming Outlook email.
- **Ringtone Selection**: Full integration with the Android system ringtone picker.
- **Material 3 UI**: Modern "Glassmorphism" interface designed for high readability over custom backgrounds.
- **Test Mode**: Immediate testing button to verify sound and vibration settings.

## 🛠 How It Works

The app uses an Android `NotificationListenerService` to monitor incoming notifications. When a notification arrives from `com.microsoft.office.outlook`:
1. It checks if the master toggle is **ON**.
2. It verifies if the current time is within your **Active Window**.
3. It ignores "Summary" or "Header" notifications to prevent double-ringing.
4. It triggers a persistent ringtone and vibration until you tap **"Stop Ringing"** from the notification.

## 📥 Setup & Installation

To make this app "Mission Critical" reliable, follow these steps after installation:

1. **Grant Notification Access**: Required to "see" Outlook notifications.
2. **Post Notification Permission**: Required to show the "Stop" button.
3. **Disable Battery Optimization**: This is **crucial**. Android often kills background services at night. Whitelist this app to ensure it stays alive.
4. **Volume & DND**: Ensure your ringer volume is up and DND is either off or configured to allow this app's alerts.

1. Clone the repository.
2. Open in **Android Studio**.
3. Sync Gradle and build.
4. (Optional) Replace `app/src/main/res/drawable/background.png` with your own background image.

## ⚠️ Important Note on Spam

The app relies on the notifications generated by the official Outlook app. If an email is filtered into the **Spam/Junk** folder by Outlook, it will not generate a notification, and therefore this app will not ring. Ensure important contacts are on your "Safe Senders" list.
